## 1. Revising an agreement

- [x] 1.1 Migration: `agreement_revisions` (numbered per engagement, reason required) and `agreement_revision_outcomes` (one per revision: accepted, declined, withdrawn), both refused any update; `revision_id` on proposal versions and agreements; several agreements per engagement, at most one first and one per revision; inserts refused for an answer by a non-party and for an agreement lacking both parties' acceptance of its version
- [x] 1.2 Service: open a revision (agreement, active or completion pending; one open at a time; terms must change something), counter-versions through the ordinary proposal route, answers through the ordinary response route, decline and withdraw end it, the second acceptance of one version records the new agreement in force without moving the engagement; completion neither asked for nor confirmed while one is open; every step on an engagement serialised by a row lock
- [x] 1.3 HTTP: the two new routes in the catalogue, the negotiation view and the export carrying `agreements` and `revisions`, and the other party notified at each step through the engagement's conversation
- [x] 1.4 Tests: in force only on mutual acceptance of one version, one at a time, blocked completion, withdraw by opener only, declined and withdrawn leave the agreement unchanged, records immutable, no answer on another's behalf, export carries the history; each guard sabotaged and seen to fail
- [x] 1.5 Interface: Propose a revision from the terms in force with a reason, the change shown against the agreement in force part by part, accept, decline, ask for a change, counter, withdraw, and the history timeline; English and Swahili
- [x] 1.6 Documentation: the directory guide's revisions section, export fields and routes; the integration guide's directory agreements section
